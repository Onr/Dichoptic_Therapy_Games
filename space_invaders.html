Prepare your practice

Wear your red/green or red/blue glasses. With both eyes open, adjust until both symbols are clear and similarly noticeable.

Use only with clinician guidance for a stable, diagnosed condition. Stop for pain, nausea, headache, or worsening double vision.
Use pure black first, with both purity controls at 100%. Check one lens at a time. If a colored half still leaks through, lower that half’s visibility. If needed, choose the adjustable background and slowly add the leaking color until its outline blends into the background for the wrong eye while staying visible for the intended eye.
