# Dichoptic Therapy for Amblyopia

Dichoptic therapy is an innovative approach to treating amblyopia (commonly known as "lazy eye") by presenting different images to each eye simultaneously. This method encourages the brain to integrate inputs from both eyes, enhancing binocular vision and reducing suppression of the weaker eye.

---

## 🎯 What is Dichoptic Therapy?

Dichoptic therapy involves delivering distinct visual stimuli to each eye. Typically, the amblyopic (weaker) eye receives high-contrast images, while the dominant eye is presented with lower-contrast images. This contrast adjustment compels the brain to utilize both eyes together, promoting neural plasticity and improving visual function.

This technique is often implemented using red-green or red-blue anaglyph glasses, where each lens filters specific images to the corresponding eye.

---

## 🧠 How It Works

1. **Separate Stimuli Presentation**: Each eye is shown different images or parts of an image.
2. **Contrast Balancing**: The dominant eye's image contrast is reduced to encourage the use of the amblyopic eye.
3. **Binocular Integration**: The brain learns to merge inputs from both eyes, enhancing depth perception and visual acuity.([Vivid Vision][1])

This method contrasts with traditional patching, which occludes the dominant eye entirely, potentially leading to compliance issues and limited binocular improvement.([ResearchGate][2])

## 🎮 Therapeutic Games and Tools

Several games and applications have been developed to facilitate dichoptic therapy:

* **Dig Rush**: An engaging game where players guide characters through challenges, requiring input from both eyes. Studies have shown significant improvements in visual acuity with its use.

* **Tetris Variants**: Modified versions of Tetris present different game elements to each eye, promoting binocular coordination.([Wikipedia][3])

* **AmblyoPlay**: A digital platform offering various exercises tailored for amblyopia treatment, utilizing dichoptic principles.

* **Vivid Vision**: A virtual reality system providing immersive experiences designed to improve binocular vision through dichoptic stimulation.

---

## ✅ Benefits of Dichoptic Therapy

* **Enhanced Visual Acuity**: Improves the sharpness of vision in the amblyopic eye.

* **Improved Stereoacuity**: Enhances depth perception by training both eyes to work together.([Cook Vision Therapy][4])

* **Reduced Suppression**: Decreases the brain's tendency to ignore input from the weaker eye.

* **Higher Compliance**: Gamified approaches increase patient engagement compared to traditional methods like patching.
